    Model. Torques are taken about the fulcrum: τ = rmg, with counter-clockwise torque from masses to the left of the fulcrum and clockwise torque from masses to its right. The stick is a uniform 100.0 cm ruler, so its whole weight M acts at its center of gravity. Every hanging assembly carries the hanger clamp mass. Equilibrium means Στccw = Στcw; the reading shown is the percent difference between the two sides.
