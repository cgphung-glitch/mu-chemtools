Work & Power Lab · Student

Cart up an incline · W = F d · P = W/t

Procedure · moving up the incline

Pull the cart up the board with a hanging weight

    Held still, the cart is held by friction; it only breaks free once the hanging weight beats mcart g sinθ + f. Add weight until it moves, then time the run.

    Incline & cart

    The angle is set by the board: sinθ = H/L = 0.250, so θ = 14.5°. The rise the cart makes is h = d sinθ = 12.50 cm. Drag the ● stop mark on the board to change d.

    Hanger & weights

    Total mass 250 g = 0.250 kg

    Total mass = mass of hanger + mass added, and the worksheet takes the pull on the cart to be its weight, F = mtotal g.

    Model & timing

    125 g on the hanger
    Show the force arrows on the cart

    Ideal treats the wheels as frictionless, so the pulling weight's work lands entirely in kinetic and potential energy. Realistic adds rolling resistance f = μ mcart g cosθ to the wheels, so part of the work goes into non-conservative work f d. Both models include the cart's weight along the slope, which is why the cart still needs weight on the hanger to climb.

    Record sheet · part A

    Model. The cart is timed from the start mark at the bottom to the stop mark a distance d up the board, so its kinematics are vavg = d/t, a = 2d/t² and vf = 2d/t. The worksheet takes the force pulling the cart to be the hanging weight, F = mtotal g, giving W = F d and P = W/t; the cart's own weight along the slope means a real cart needs enough hanging weight to break free. Conservative work is the cart's change in potential energy, Wcons = ΔPE = mcart g h, and the kinetic energy change is ΔKE = ½ m vf². Two simplifications are worth knowing: a real string pulls with T = mhanger(g − a), a little less than the hanging weight, and the hanging assembly speeds up too, so its kinetic energy belongs in any energy balance that is meant to close. Every timed reading, photo-gate or stopwatch, carries about ±0.5 % of measurement scatter, so repeated trials of one mass come out close but not identical, and the energy balance closes to within a tolerance rather than to zero.
